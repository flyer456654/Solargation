T-Mobile / Phoebus Puerto Rico renewable energy agreement

Puerto Rico Renewable energy certificates Wheeling Program Solargation® technology

T-Mobile has signed an agreement with The Phoebus Fund for a future solar project in Puerto Rico designed to match 100% of T-Mobile’s local electricity use with renewable energy, supported by renewable energy certificates.

The project remains in early development and is expected to leverage Puerto Rico’s Wheeling Program. The proposed project would use Phoebus’ patented Solargation®™ technology to generate solar power while supporting agricultural use.

Planning is guided by Phoebus’ agriculture advisory board, reinforcing the company’s focus on renewable energy infrastructure that can preserve and enhance productive agricultural land.

Solargation® is positioned as more than a power project: it is a combined-use platform for solar generation, irrigation, land stewardship, and agricultural productivity.

Energy alignment

The agreement targets a renewable match for T-Mobile’s local Puerto Rico electricity use through a future solar project and renewable energy certificates.

Puerto Rico market structure

The project is expected to use the Puerto Rico Wheeling Program, supporting delivery of renewable power through the local grid framework.

Agriculture-first design

Phoebus’ patented Solargation® approach is intended to produce clean energy while continuing to support agricultural use of the land.
